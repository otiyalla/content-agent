# First followers

A feed post from an account nobody follows reaches almost no one, so the first followers have to come from people
who already know Zenlot or the founder. The API can't follow, like or comment for us, and automating those gets
accounts restricted. These steps are done by hand, in about 15 minutes a day.

**Start:** 3 followers (23 November 2026). **Goal:** 1,000. The Sunday email shows progress. Until the goal, at least
2 of each week's 5 posts ask people to follow, naming the series.

## 1. Invite people who already know you (once, this week)

Post these from the founder's own accounts. Edit them so they sound like you. The link to use is
`https://www.instagram.com/zenlot_app/`.

**Personal Instagram (Story, with a link sticker or a mention of @zenlot_app)**

> I started Zenlot as an Excel sheet to stop repeating my own trading mistakes. It's now a free journaling app, and
> it has an Instagram: @zenlot_app. Risk maths, checklists and how to review your trades. Follow if you trade forex.

**LinkedIn or Facebook**

> I noticed I made more errors when I wasn't following my own rules, so I started writing every trade down in a
> spreadsheet. That spreadsheet became Zenlot, a free forex trading journal for iOS and Android.
>
> We've started posting on Instagram: risk maths with the working shown, pre-trade checklists and how to review a
> week of trades. No signals and no trade calls. https://www.instagram.com/zenlot_app/
>
> Educational content, not financial advice.

**Personal X account**

> I built Zenlot, a free forex trading journal. We now post risk maths and trade-review routines on Instagram, no
> signals: https://www.instagram.com/zenlot_app/ Not financial advice.

**Direct message, only to people you know who trade**

> Hey, I've started an Instagram for Zenlot, the trading journal I built. If it's useful to you I'd love a follow,
> and tell me honestly what you think: instagram.com/zenlot_app

Send these from your personal account only. @zenlot_app never messages people first: its bio promises "we never
DM you for money", and a DM from a forex brand looks like a scam.

Also add the Instagram link to your email signature and to the profiles in [PROFILE.md](PROFILE.md) section 5.

## 2. Warm up the account (15 minutes a day for 2 weeks)

From **24 November to 7 December 2026**, signed in as @zenlot_app. Automatic posting is already on, so this runs
alongside it.

| Each day | How |
|---|---|
| Reply to every comment | Use the digest email and [COMMUNITY.md](COMMUNITY.md). |
| Leave 3 useful comments | On recent posts by forex educators. Add a point or ask a question. Don't mention Zenlot. |
| Watch and like about 10 posts | Trading psychology, journaling and risk topics. |
| Follow up to 3 accounts | Ones you'd really read: educators and journaling traders. |

Only engage with accounts that post education and have **no** signals or paid groups, **no** broker referral links
and **no** profit screenshots. Same rule as `content/x-targets.json`.

Once or twice a week, share that day's post to your Story and answer any replies. Risk-maths posts already get one
automatically.

## 3. Ask app users

An optional "Follow us on Instagram" link in the Zenlot app, shown after a trade verdict. This needs a change in the
app's own repository, so it ships with an app release.

## Never

- Buy followers, likes or comments, or join engagement pods.
- Follow people to get follow-backs, then unfollow them.
- Use bots or scripts that like, follow, comment or message.
- Run giveaways that require a follow, or promise results for following.

## Check progress (Sundays)

| Followers | What to do next |
|---|---|
| under 50 | Repeat step 1 with anyone you haven't asked yet. Nothing else matters as much. |
| 50–200 | Keep the daily routine. Look at "Follows" per post in the Sunday email. |
| 200–1,000 | Story reshares (item 19) and Reels (item 5) start to pay off. |
| 1,000+ | The planner stops requiring follow posts. |
