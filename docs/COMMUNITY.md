# Comments

New comments on @zenlot_app's recent posts are collected several times a day (about 30 minutes, 2 hours
and 6 hours after the 13:00 UTC post) and emailed as one digest, with a suggested reply for each.
**Nothing is posted automatically.** Read the draft, change it if needed, and reply in the Instagram app.

The rules the drafts follow live in `src/content/community-playbook.ts`. This page is the same playbook for people.

## Urgent (top of the digest)

| Comment | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Distress** ("I lost everything", hopelessness) | Reply with care first. Suggest stepping away from the charts and talking to someone they trust. If they might be in danger, encourage contacting local emergency services. Don't mention Zenlot or teach. Check back on the thread later. |
| **Impersonation** (an account with "zenlot" in its name that isn't @zenlot_app) | Reply under the comment: our only account is @zenlot_app and we never DM for money. Then report the account: profile → ⋯ → Report → Pretending to be someone → Me or my business. |
| **Scam** (DM bait, WhatsApp or Telegram, "signals", "account managers", fund recovery, phone numbers) | Hide the comment (swipe left → hide). Don't engage. If followers might fall for it, reply with the safety line. |

## Everything else

| Comment | Reply |
|---|---|
| "What should I buy?" / "Is EURUSD going up?" | Kindly decline to call trades. Offer the process instead: decide the risk first, size from the stop, write down the reason. |
| Questions about Zenlot | Answer factually: free on iOS and Android, link in bio. Only mention features that exist. |
| Questions about the post | Answer directly with the maths or method. |
| Disagreement | Thank them, take their best point seriously, and clarify calmly. |
| Praise | A short, genuine thank-you. |
| Spam | No reply; hide it. |

## Always

- Never give trade calls, price predictions or opinions on someone's trade.
- Never ask anyone to DM, and never post contact details other than the link in bio.
- Every draft goes through the same brand check as posts. A draft that fails is marked in the digest.
